# Curatorial instructions — transferable principles without derivative maps

## Hierarchy of evidence

**Primary standard:** Five existing source-pinned CyberSand gallery screenshots (Deepworks, GEN-007 gallery 4, Neon Reliquary Descent 04, Blacksite Nine, GEN-007 atlas). Judge *macro composition, physical rendering, microdetail, colour/value hierarchy, narrative place-making and scale coherence* against these actual references.

**Secondary invention pool:** Original TokenSplurge works are cues for techniques, metaphors, geometry and spatial relationships, **not** accepted CyberSand visuals. Their browser-rendered source is independent of the CyberSand renderer. Draw from multiple dissimilar principles without reproducing a source composition.

**Concept studies:** LINEAGE/SALVAGE examples may suggest algorithms or topology. A toy automaton does not establish geology, material conservation or simulation stability.

## Required annotation for a promoted source

Every card supplies (1) source title/path and immutable commit + blob SHA, (2) visible/source-grounded design principle, (3) what can be *transformed* into CyberSand with native-cell/material constraints, (4) explicit elements not to copy, (5) relevant map-pack themes, and (6) real render/capture evidence and limitations.

Score candidates 0–5 for originality, strong silhouette, detail hierarchy, technical and spatial ingenuity, environmental narrative, coherence and cross-theme diversity. Reject or downgrade pieces whose success depends on copying a prior layout, whose screenshot cannot be inspected, or whose purported material behaviour is not evidenced.

## Creative distance — production and curation

**Good direction:** "Develop a buried mechanical observatory where different geological eras constrain the transport network, with one visually legible focal structure, multi-scale erosion detail and distinct dark/light zones."

**Bad direction:** "Reproduce The Midnight Railway inside CyberSand, with its same tunnels, hubs and warm palette."

For new concept briefs:
- Start from an **environmental question or tension**, not the name of a reference artwork.
- Let the worker select fresh scale, viewpoint, architecture, palette, materials and interaction. Preserve the ability to change direction substantially.
- Treat references as separate axes (e.g. rivers for drainage *logic*, a city for settlement *rhythm*, an observatory for lighting *discipline*) rather than as ready-made layouts.
- Do not trace identifiable waterways, rail topology, facades, props, typography, character silhouettes, UI styling, colour keys or encoded procedural expressions.
- When a source stands out because of JavaScript simulation, identify the general emergent behaviour and either author a different technique or keep it purely visual. Never misstate that CyberSand supports the same physics.
- Respect actual native material IDs, active vs fixed materials, overwrite order, collision/negative space and any provenance/qualification contract. Synthetic-looking light is not automatically a shader-emissive or source-light simulation.

## Future workers and boundaries

It may ignore the obvious solution to preserve experimentation. Do not rewrite the unrestricted art workers.

**Visual curator (proposed, NOT scheduled here):** Review all streams, including unplanned and unusual outputs. Select evidence-backed examples, update this gallery and short design prompts on a *separate documentation branch*. Reject near-duplicates. Never edit the currently active CyberSand `#454` world-pack source branch, or merge/create competing map implementations.

**CyberSand atlas workers:** May consult this library voluntarily for novel ideas, but their existing ownership matrix, exact source and renderer gates, expected materials and human graphical acceptance requirements take precedence. Keep the benchmark images mandatory for comparison and the TokenSplurge cards optional inspiration.

## Independence and provenance

The library is a design instrument, not an acceptance report. It must not be used to assert that a CyberSand world pack already exceeds the five baseline reference captures, or to bypass source-pinned Godot testing. Note screenshot platform, hash, revision, generation method, limitations and whether an image is source-code evidence, browser evidence or Godot-native evidence.
